Pet Phonics: Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

The short version. Pet Phonics is a reading game for young children. It is built so that your child's information stays on your iPhone.

  • We do not collect any personal information from you or your child.
  • The app has no accounts, no adverts, no tracking and no third-party analytics or advertising code.
  • Your child's voice is turned into words on the iPhone itself. It is not recorded, not saved and not sent to us or anyone else.
  • Game progress and your child's first name are saved only on your iPhone.

Who we are

Pet Phonics is published by Mindcom Media Ltd, a company registered in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or about privacy in the app, email tim@theboost.ai.

Information we collect

None. Pet Phonics does not send any information about you or your child to us. The app does not connect to any server of ours, and it contains no third-party software that collects data.

Information kept on your iPhone

The app saves a small amount of information on your device so the game can work. It stays on the device and we cannot see it.

  • Your child's first name, if you choose to type one in. It is used to personalise the app and the reminders it shows you. You can leave it blank.
  • Game progress: levels completed, stars, coins, pets, and which words and sounds your child has read.
  • Your settings, such as whether listening with the microphone is switched on.

The microphone and speech recognition

With your permission, Pet Phonics listens while your child reads aloud so that each word can light up as it is read. The sound is turned into words by Apple's speech recognition running on the iPhone itself. The app requires on-device recognition, so the audio is not sent to us, to Apple or to anyone else. The audio is not recorded or saved, and the words heard are discarded once they have been checked against the word on screen.

The microphone is optional. You can choose "Play without the microphone" when you set the app up, and you can switch listening on or off at any time in the parent area or in the iPhone's Settings.

On some iPhones, iOS may download a speech model from Apple the first time on-device recognition is used. That download is handled by Apple and contains nothing about your child.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can show you a reminder to come back the next day, and a note when your child has learned a new sound. These are created and scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server, and no notification token or other information is sent to us.

Subscriptions and purchases

Pet Phonics offers a yearly subscription with a free trial. Purchases are made through Apple's App Store using your Apple ID. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your name, payment card details or billing address. The app only learns from Apple whether a subscription is active on the device. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous, combined statistics such as download numbers and crash reports. These do not identify you or your child.

Children's privacy

Pet Phonics is made for young children and is set up by a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, or from anyone else. The app contains:

  • no adverts;
  • no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code;
  • no links out to websites or social media in the child's part of the app;
  • no chat, accounts or way for a child to share information.

The subscription screen and the parent area are for adults. The parent area is behind a parental gate.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone, because we do not collect any.

Deleting your information

Everything the app saves is on your iPhone, so you are in control of it:

  • In the parent area, choose "Start again from the first egg" and then "Delete everything" to erase your child's name and all progress.
  • Deleting the app from your iPhone also removes everything it saved.

We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law, and under laws such as COPPA and the CCPA in the United States, you have rights over personal information that an organisation holds about you or your child, including the rights to see it, correct it and have it deleted. Because we do not collect or hold any personal information from the app, we have none to provide, correct or delete. If you email us, we will use your email address and message only to reply to you.

If you are in the UK and are unhappy with how we have handled a privacy question, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches you. If a change would mean collecting personal information, we will ask for a parent's consent where the law requires it.
