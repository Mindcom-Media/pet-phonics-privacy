Pet Phonics: Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

The short version. Pet Phonics is a reading game for young children. It is built so that your child's information stays on your iPhone or iPad.

  • The app does not send any personal information about you or your child to us or to anyone else.
  • The app has no accounts, no adverts, no tracking and no third-party analytics or advertising code.
  • Your child's voice is turned into words on the iPhone or iPad itself. It is not recorded, not saved and not sent to us or anyone else.
  • Game progress and the player's first name are saved only on your iPhone or iPad, and you can delete them at any time.
  • Questions? Email hello@getunlocked.ai.

Who we are

Pet Phonics is published by Mindcom Media Ltd, a company registered in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or about privacy in the app, email hello@getunlocked.ai.

This policy explains what happens to information when you and your child use the app. Our Terms of Use explain the rules for using it.

Information we collect

None through the app. Pet Phonics does not send any information about you or your child to us. The app does not connect to any server of ours, and it contains no third-party software that collects data.

The only information we ever receive is what you choose to send us yourself, for example if you email us for help. See "If you email us" below.

Information kept on your iPhone or iPad

The app saves a small amount of information on your device so the game can work. It stays on the device and we cannot see it.

  • A first name, if you choose to type one in. The app asks who is playing so that it can use the name in the game and in the reminders it shows you. You can leave it blank.
  • Game progress: levels completed, stars, coins, stickers, pets, buildings, and which words and sounds your child has read, including the words that need more practice.
  • Your settings, such as whether listening with the microphone is switched on.

If you back up your iPhone or iPad to iCloud or to a computer, this information is included in that backup, in the same way as other apps' data. Backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

The microphone and speech recognition

With your permission, Pet Phonics listens while your child reads aloud so that each word can light up as it is read. The sound is turned into words by Apple's speech recognition running on the iPhone or iPad itself. The app requires on-device recognition, so the audio is not sent to us, to Apple or to anyone else. The audio is not recorded or saved, and the words heard are thrown away once they have been checked against the word on screen.

The microphone is optional. If you say no when your device asks, the game still works: your child taps the sounds of each word instead of reading aloud. You can switch listening on or off at any time in the grown-ups' area of the app, or in your device's Settings app.

On some devices, iOS or iPadOS may download a speech model from Apple the first time on-device recognition is used. That download is handled by Apple and contains nothing about your child.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can show you a reminder to come back the next day, a note when your child has learned a new sound, and a number on the app's icon showing how many things are waiting in the game. The reminders can include the first name you typed in. They are created and scheduled on your iPhone or iPad. They are not sent from a server, and no notification token or other information is sent to us.

Notifications are optional and the game works without them. You can turn them off at any time in your device's Settings app.

Downloads and Apple

Pet Phonics is free to download from Apple's App Store and has no in-app purchases. Apple's handling of your information when you use the App Store is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may give us anonymous, combined statistics such as download numbers and crash reports. These do not identify you or your child.

Children's privacy

Pet Phonics is a game for young children that a parent or guardian sets up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, or from anyone else, through the app. The app contains:

  • no adverts;
  • no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code;
  • no links out to websites or social media in the child's part of the app;
  • no chat, accounts or way for a child to share information.

A grown-up sets the app up before the iPhone or iPad is handed to the child. The grown-ups' area, where the settings, the email address, this policy and the "Delete all data" button are, is behind a parental gate: a multiplication question that only a grown-up is likely to answer.

If we ever learn that a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, we will delete it.

A note for children

Hello! When you read to your pets, the phone or iPad listens to you. It does not keep your voice, and it does not send it to anyone. Your pets and your coins stay on this phone or iPad. If you are not sure about anything, ask a grown-up.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone, because the app does not collect any.

If you email us

If you email us, we use your email address and your message only to reply to you and to help with your question. We do this because we have a legitimate interest in answering you. Your email is held by the email service we use, which keeps it secure under its own data protection commitments. We keep emails only as long as we need them to help you, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your emails at any time.

How long information is kept

  • Information in the app stays on your iPhone or iPad until you delete it, or delete the app.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as we need them to help you.

Deleting your information

Everything the app saves is on your iPhone or iPad, so you are in control of it:

  • In the grown-ups' area, choose "Delete all data…" and then "Delete everything". This erases the saved first name, all progress, and any reminders waiting to be shown.
  • Deleting the app from your device also removes everything it saved.

We hold no copy of anything saved in the app, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changing your mind

You can take back any permission you have given at any time. Switch off listening in the grown-ups' area, or turn off the microphone, speech recognition or notifications for Pet Phonics in your device's Settings app. The game keeps working without them.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law, and under laws such as COPPA and the CCPA in the United States, you have rights over personal information that an organisation holds about you or your child, including the rights to see it, correct it and have it deleted. Because we do not collect or hold any personal information from the app, we have none to provide, correct or delete. If you have emailed us, you can ask to see or delete those emails.

If you are in the UK and are unhappy with how we have handled a privacy question, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. If you are in the EU, you can complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches you. If a change would mean collecting personal information, we will ask for a parent's consent where the law requires it.
