Pet Phonics: Help and Contact

For parents and guardians

Need help? Email hello@getunlocked.ai. Tell us what happened and which iPhone or iPad you use, and we will get back to you.

The grown-ups' area

Tap the gear button at the top of the game, then answer the question. There you can see your child's progress, switch listening on or off, email us, read the privacy policy and the terms of use, and delete all data.

The app does not hear my child

  • Check that the microphone and speech recognition are allowed for Pet Phonics in the Settings app of your iPhone or iPad.
  • Turn the sound up, and play somewhere quiet.
  • The first time listening is used, the device may need a moment to get its speech model ready.
  • Without the microphone, your child can still play by tapping the sounds of each word.

Starting again

In the grown-ups' area, choose "Delete all data…" and then "Delete everything". This erases the saved first name and all progress on this device. It cannot be undone.
